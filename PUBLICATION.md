# Publication after review

These notes, documentation patches and explanatory comments are AI-assisted technical drafts for independent human review. No upstream issue, comment, fork or PR has been submitted.

Bevy's [AI policy](https://bevy.org/learn/contribute/policies/ai/) requires independently human-written public prose, including documentation, issues, PR descriptions and communication; human commit authorship, ownership of every submitted line and disclosure of assistance are also required. This includes rustdoc and comments in the prepared patches. Further AI review does not replace those obligations.

wgpu's [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/blob/trunk/CONTRIBUTING.md) permits human-owned assisted code with disclosure and human descriptions; its [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu/blob/trunk/AGENTS.md) additionally rejects fully agentic PRs. Recheck the target project's current policy and trackers before publication.

Before an approved upstream submission, inspect the final diff, commit authors/committers and messages. Remove technical-draft headers and tool footers; write the required disclosure independently. Review any agent-name matches in context, since legitimate crate names can also match. Do not hide assistance or copy the prepared prose upstream.
